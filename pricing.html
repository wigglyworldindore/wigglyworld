Pricing & Timings FAQ

What are WigglyWorld's charges in Indore?
WigglyWorld charges ₹300 per hour on weekdays (Mon-Fri) and ₹350 per hour on weekends and holidays (Sat, Sun). Each extra 30 minutes is ₹100.
What are WigglyWorld's timings?
WigglyWorld is open every day, including weekends and public holidays, from 1:30 PM to 10:00 PM.
Is there a membership or discount plan?
Every visit includes a ₹100 discount coupon for your next visit. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 91792 10010 for current membership and multi-visit package options.
Up to what age is WigglyWorld for?
WigglyWorld is for children aged 1 to 10 years. Socks are mandatory, and diapers are required for children under 4.
